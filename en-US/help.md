# How to use CarryOver

CarryOver helps you capture links, text, and moments of interest now, then review and act on them later from the device that fits the work.

## Save content

### Open any app

Start in Safari, Notes, a social app, or any app that can share text or URLs.

### Choose supported content

Choose the text, link, image or post you want to keep. What can be shared depends on the source app.

### Use Share

In a compatible app, open the system Share menu and choose CarryOver. On iPhone and iPad, check More if it is not visible. On Mac, availability also depends on the source app, content type and system extension settings.

### Review and save

In the share form, review the content and optionally edit the title or set a reminder. Choose Save to add it to your inbox.

### Add a note manually

Open Add Share in CarryOver to record an idea or paste content, then save it to your inbox.

## Manage your list

### Open the item or its source

Select an item to view or edit it. If a source URL is available, use the source action to open it in the browser.

### Mark it done

Use the completion button after handling an item. Only completed items move to History; unfinished items do not expire.

### Restore from History

Use the restore button beside a completed item to return it to the inbox.

### Delete with confirmation

On iPhone and iPad, swipe left on an item and choose Delete. On Mac, use the delete action. Confirm before the item is removed.

## Sync and troubleshooting

### Use the same iCloud account

Sync works across devices signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud available.

### Retry failed sync

If an item shows a sync issue, tap the status icon to retry.

### Check iCloud storage

If the app reports sync success but another device does not update, iCloud may be out of storage or waiting for background upload.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I share to CarryOver?

In a compatible app, open the system Share menu and choose CarryOver. On iPhone and iPad, check More if it is not visible. On Mac, availability also depends on the source app, content type and system extension settings.

### Why did sync not appear on another device immediately?

iCloud sync can be delayed by network status, Low Power Mode, or insufficient iCloud storage. Keep the app installed on both devices and allow the system time to finish background sync.

### Does CarryOver require an account?

No separate CarryOver account is required. Sync uses Apple's iCloud services when available.

### Do unfinished items disappear after a few days?

No. Unfinished items remain in the inbox regardless of age. Marking an item done moves it to History; deleting removes it.

### Can I search my saved content?

Yes. Search titles, saved text and source URLs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, including completed items in History.

### Will Later actually remind me?

Yes. Set a future date and time and allow notifications for CarryOver in system settings. The system delivers a local notification; Focus and notification settings may silence it. Tapping it opens the matching item.

### Can I continue a completed item?

Yes. Open History and use the item's restore button to return it to the inbox.

### Is there a daily save limit?

The current version has no daily save limit and does not require a separate Pro upgrade. Check the App Store for availability and pricing in your region.
