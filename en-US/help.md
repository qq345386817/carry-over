# How to use CarryOver

Pick up on Mac with the links, quotes and images you saved on iPhone or iPad. If you forget a title, search for the source domain, a phrase you saved or the note you left yourself.

## Save content

### Choose supported content

Choose the text, link, image or post you want to keep. What can be shared depends on the source app.

### Use Share

In a compatible app, open the system Share menu and choose CarryOver. On iPhone and iPad, check More if it is not visible. On Mac, availability also depends on the source app, content type and system extension settings.

### Review and save

Add “Check sample size” to the walking study, or leave the note blank. In the share form, review the content and optionally edit the title or set a reminder. Choose Save to add it to your inbox.

### Add a note manually

Open Add Share in CarryOver to record an idea or paste content, then save it to your inbox.

## Manage your list

### Open the item or its source

Search the source domain, a phrase from the shared text, a personal note or a topic in the inbox or History. Open the result to check its source; opening it doesn’t mark it done.

### Keep important items in Favorites

Favorite an unfinished item to find it in the Favorites filter. It stays in your inbox until you mark it complete.

### Mark it done

Use the completion button after handling an item. Only completed items move to History; unfinished items do not expire.

### Restore from History

Use the restore button beside a completed item to return it to the inbox.

### Delete with confirmation

On iPhone and iPad, swipe left on an item and choose Delete. On Mac, use the delete action. Confirm before the item is removed. Trash: Deleted items are kept for 30 days. You can restore them before they are permanently deleted.

### Export Markdown

Item details › More actions › Export Markdown. To use it in your writing, export that item’s source and notes as a Markdown file.

## Sync and troubleshooting

### Use the same Apple Account

Sign in to the same Apple Account on iPhone, iPad and Mac, enable iCloud and connect to the internet to sync saved items.

### Save locally first

Saving adds the item to this device before cross-device sync finishes. If it has not arrived elsewhere, check the sync status, network and available iCloud storage.

### Retry when needed

Use the retry action on a failed item. On the other device, open CarryOver and refresh from iCloud. Network and iCloud conditions can delay updates.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I share to CarryOver?

In a compatible app, open the system Share menu and choose CarryOver. On iPhone and iPad, check More if it is not visible. On Mac, availability also depends on the source app, content type and system extension settings.

### Why did sync not appear on another device immediately?

iCloud sync can be delayed by network status, Low Power Mode, or insufficient iCloud storage. Keep the app installed on both devices and allow the system time to finish background sync.

### Does CarryOver require an account?

No separate CarryOver account is required. Sync uses Apple's iCloud services when available.

### What does CarryOver save from other apps?

It saves the text, links or images that the source app actually shares. A social post may be shared as a link rather than its full content. Opening a link may require internet access or a login.

### Can I save now and sync later?

Items are saved locally first. Cross-device sync needs the same Apple Account, iCloud enabled and an internet connection. Check the sync status and retry if needed; syncing is not guaranteed to be immediate.

### Can I find a saved item without its title?

Search the source domain, a phrase from the shared text, a personal note or a topic in the inbox or History. Open the result to check its source; opening it doesn’t mark it done.

### Can I read a saved article offline?

For supported public HTTPS articles, you can save a plain-text copy to read offline on this device. Article copies stay on the device where you create them. Item details › More actions › Create article copy

### Do unfinished items disappear after a few days?

Unfinished items stay in your inbox regardless of age. Completed items move to History.

### Will Later actually remind me?

Yes. Set a future date and time and allow notifications for CarryOver in system settings. The system delivers a local notification; Focus and notification settings may silence it. Tapping it opens the matching item.

### Can I continue a completed item?

Yes. Open History and use the item's restore button to return it to the inbox.

### Is there a daily save limit?

The current version has no daily save limit and does not require a separate Pro upgrade. Check the App Store for availability and pricing in your region.
